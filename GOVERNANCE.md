# Governance

Crustates is maintained by Crustates BV.

Decisions are recorded as ADRs in
[`crustates/crustates/docs/adr/`](https://github.com/crustates/crustates/tree/development/docs/adr)
and discussed in public pull requests.

Maintainers are listed in the
[`@crustates/maintainers`](https://github.com/orgs/crustates/teams/maintainers)
team.
